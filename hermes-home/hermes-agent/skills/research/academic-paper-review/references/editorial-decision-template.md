# Editorial Decision Template

用于合成所有审稿人报告为最终编辑决定。

---

## Template

```markdown
# 📋 Editorial Decision

## Manuscript Information
- **Title**: [论文标题]
- **Decision Date**: [日期]
- **Review Round**: [Round N]

---

## Decision: [Accept / Minor Revision / Major Revision / Reject]

---

## Reviewer Summary

| Reviewer | Role | Recommendation | Confidence |
|----------|------|---------------|------------|
| EIC | Journal Editor | [Accept/Minor/Major/Reject] | [1-5] |
| Reviewer 1 | Methodology Expert | [Accept/Minor/Major/Reject] | [1-5] |
| Reviewer 2 | Domain Expert | [Accept/Minor/Major/Reject] | [1-5] |
| Devil's Advocate | Logic Challenger | [Accept/Minor/Major/Reject] | [1-5] |

---

## Consensus Analysis

### Points of Agreement
1. [All reviewers agree on...]
2. [...]

### Points of Disagreement
- **Reviewer X**: [Viewpoint]
- **Reviewer Y**: [Different viewpoint]
- **Resolution**: [How to arbitrate]

---

## Required Revisions (Must Fix)

| # | Item | Source | Severity | Section |
|---|------|--------|----------|---------|
| R1 | [Description] | [Reviewer] | Critical | [Section] |
| R2 | [Description] | [Reviewer] | Major | [Section] |

---

## Suggested Revisions (Recommended)

| # | Item | Source | Section |
|---|------|--------|---------|
| S1 | [Description] | [Reviewer] | [Section] |

---

## Revision Roadmap

### Priority 1 — Critical (must address for acceptance)
- [ ] R1: [Description + how to fix]
- [ ] R2: [Description + how to fix]

### Priority 2 — Major (strongly recommended)
- [ ] R3: [Description + how to fix]

### Priority 3 — Minor (quality improvements)
- [ ] S1: [Description]
```
