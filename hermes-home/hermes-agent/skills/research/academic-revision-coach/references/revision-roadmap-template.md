## Revision Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Critical Fixes (P1 — Must Do)
These are blocking issues. Address FIRST.

**C1: [Title]**
- **Problem**: [What the reviewer identified]
- **Required Action**: [Concrete step to fix]
- **Target Section**: §[N]
- **Acceptance Criteria**: [How to verify the fix]
- **Estimated Effort**: [hours/days]

### Phase 2: Important Improvements (P2 — Should Do)
These improve quality but aren't blocking.

**C[N]: [Title]**
- [Same format as above]

### Phase 3: Minor Touch-ups (P3 — Quick Fix)
Editorial and minor issues.

**C[N]: [Title]**
- **Action**: [Brief fix description]

## Pre-submission Checklist

- [ ] All Major comments addressed (Phase 1 complete)
- [ ] All Minor comments addressed or explicitly responded to
- [ ] All Editorial comments fixed
- [ ] Response letter drafted for each reviewer
- [ ] Changes tracked (highlighted in manuscript or separate change list)
- [ ] Re-read revised sections for internal consistency
